## Long Vowels — Compact Notes

Long vowels usually **say their letter name**: **a, e, i, o, u**.

| Vowel | Sound | Mouth tip | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | /eɪ/ | Mouth wide, then closes slightly | say, babe, paid, wait |
| E | /iː/ | Big smile | see, need, pea, sing |
| I | /aɪ/ | Tongue/mouth glides from “ah” to “ee” | I, ride, bright |
| O | /oʊ/ | Start bigger, then small circle | row, so, grow, though |
| U | /juː/ or /uː/ | Lips come forward like “oo” | you, cute, flute, huge |

**Tip:** Move your mouth fully. Long vowels are often glides, not static sounds.

### Rules with examples
1. **Two vowels → usually say the first long, silence the second**  
   - **male, gain** → long **a**  
   - **here, near, wheat** → long **e**  
   - **coat, toe** → long **o**  
   - **fruit, juice** → long **u**  
   **Exception:** **ie** often sounds like **/iː/** (e.g., piece, field), not long **i**.

2. **Vowel + consonant + silent e → first vowel long**  
   - plane, Pete, nine, rope, note, cube, flute

3. **Single vowel at the end of a word → usually long**  
   - he, we, hi, go, no  
   **Exception:** final **a** is usually not long — e.g., **ma, ha**.

4. **I or O before two consonants → can be long**  
   - stroll, kind, gold, child

### Important takeaway
Use these clues to guess long vs short: **two vowels, silent e, final vowel, or I/O + two consonants**. Always check exceptions like **ie**.

- **U:** you, June, true, new, crew, flu, stew, juice, cute, flute, dune, blue, clue, mule, rule, cube

**Practice tip:** Repeat aloud, exaggerate mouth movement, and rewatch/redo difficult vowel sets.
