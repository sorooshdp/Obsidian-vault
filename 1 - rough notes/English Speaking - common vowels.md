
**Main difference:**  
- **Common vowels (monophthongs)** = one steady vowel sound; mouth mostly stays still.  
- **Diphthongs** = two vowel targets; mouth **moves/glides** from one sound to another.

### Common vowel sounds (one sound)

| Sound | Mouth tip | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **/iː/** | Smile, tense | eat, meet |
| **/ɪ/** | Short i; mouth partly open | it, kit, sick, pin |
| **/e/ or /ɛ/** | Short e; not as wide as /iː/ | bed, best, dress, better |
| **/æ/** | Short a; mouth wide, tongue low | at, apple, rat, matter |
| **/uː/** | Long “oo”; lips forward | pool, dune, soup, jewel |
| **/ʊ/** | Relaxed “oo”; mouth almost O | book, should, putting, put |
| **/ʌ/** | Short u; tongue relaxed/flat | but, blood, come, son |
| **/ɔː/** | “aw”; tongue pulled back | bought, daughter, long, toss |
| **/ɑː/** | “ah” like doctor’s “open and say ah” | opera, father, farm, heart |

### Diphthongs (mouth moves)

| Diphthong | Glide | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **/eɪ/** | /e/ → /ɪ/ | eight, sleigh, pain, flavor, say |
| **/oʊ/** | /o/ → /ʊ/ | toe, grow, go, goat |
| **/aɪ/** | /a/ → /ɪ/ | I, tie, cry, lime, light |
| **/aʊ/** | /a/ → /ʊ/ | proud, mouse, cow, about, now |
| **/ɔɪ/** | /ɔ/ → /ɪ/ | boy, toy, voice, noise |

### Important takeaway
- **Common vowels:** keep the sound steady.
- **Diphthongs:** let your mouth move clearly from the first vowel to the second.
- Vowels are a major part of being understood — practice aloud and exaggerate mouth movement, especially for diphthongs.
