- [ ] Use of an abbreviation improves readability, but before that, the significance should be mentioned. Also, once an abbreviation is mentioned, it is expected that it be referred to from the text.
- [ ] Authors have used symbols in abstracts; it is advisable to avoid using such things, especially when the significance of the symbols is not mentioned. Also, it is recommended to use simple sentences instead of complex ones. As a sentence without proper punctuation makes a different sense, thus causing confusion when reading.
- [ ] It is expected to discuss any terms before they are referred to.
- [ ] Starting any subsection just after a section should be avoided.
- [ ] Any claim can be made only after detailed justifications. Authors are advised to arrange or update such paragraphs accordingly.
- [ ] Captions of figures should be complete, and a brief description of the figure is advisable to understand the figure clearly. Also, improving the figures is required, as the text in the figure is too small to read.
- [ ] An equation followed by an equation number is advisable to relate the working of the proposed methods. Also, each equation should be cited and referred to from the text, or formulations should be presented.
- [ ] Methods that are mentioned in the table should be cited.
- [ ] Experimental achievement should be mentioned in the abstract
- [ ] Captions of tables and figures should be written in a summarized form.
